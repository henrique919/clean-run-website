The closeout problem CleanRun IQ is addressing

On many projects, defects still live in spreadsheets, message threads and camera rolls. A row can say an item is closed while nobody can show the picture that proves the fix. Trade hand-offs happen by text, due dates drift, and the night before practical completion becomes a scramble to reconstruct what happened.

CleanRun IQ is built around one live defect register instead of that scatter. Site teams capture the issue, assign the trade, collect rectification photos, review the fix and close the item with a history that still makes sense months later. That workflow is described in more detail on the CleanRun IQ defect management page and in the construction defect management guide.

Why photo evidence matters

A closed defect without a photo is easy to dispute and hard to defend. The product direction Henry supports treats photos as part of the item itself: the original capture, the rectification upload from the trade, and the review that allows closeout. Markup on the image helps the trade see which crack, tile or corner is meant — so less time is spent clarifying and more time is spent fixing.

That same chain is useful after handover, when questions return during the defects liability period and the team needs the record without digging through phones and email. The photo evidence workflow is how CleanRun IQ keeps that proof on the item rather than somewhere else.

Principles shaping the product

The product principles below are taken from how CleanRun IQ already describes its workflow for Australian site teams. They are the practical rules the software is meant to enforce, not marketing slogans.

  • Photo-first capture An item should not enter the register without enough visual context to act on later.
  • Named assignment and due dates Every defect should go to a clear trade owner with a date the team can see and track.
  • Evidence before closeout Rectification photos and site review come before an item is marked closed — no self-certification shortcut.
  • Audit trail per item Creation, assignment, rectification, approval and closeout should remain visible with who did what and when.
  • Handover-ready reporting The register should produce a structured record project managers can stand behind, without rebuilding evidence overnight.

What CleanRun IQ is focused on now

CleanRun IQ is being shaped with direct input from commercial construction teams who deal with defects, subcontractor sign-off, practical completion pressure and handover reporting every week. The near-term focus stays narrow: make capture fast on site, make trade response simple from a phone browser, keep review with the site manager, and lock a defensible evidence chain into the item history.
